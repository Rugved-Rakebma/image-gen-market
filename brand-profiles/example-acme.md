# ACME Tech Brand Guidelines

## Typography
- Headlines: Bold, sans-serif (Inter, SF Pro, or similar)
- Body: Regular weight, high readability
- Use generous whitespace and clear hierarchy

## Imagery Style
- Clean, modern photography with natural lighting
- Abstract geometric patterns for backgrounds
- Subtle blue-to-purple gradients for accent elements
- Tech-inspired but human-centered visuals
- Avoid stock photo cliches (handshakes, generic office scenes)

## Do
- Use the primary blue as the dominant brand color
- Include subtle geometric patterns or grid elements
- Maintain clean, uncluttered compositions
- Use amber/gold accent sparingly for CTAs and highlights
- Prefer light backgrounds with high contrast text

## Don't
- Use more than 3 colors in a single composition
- Use harsh neon colors or overly saturated palettes
- Include busy, cluttered backgrounds
- Use clip art or low-quality illustrations
- Mix too many different visual styles in one piece

## Examples
- Product screenshots on clean gradient backgrounds
- Hero banners with geometric overlays on photography
- Social cards with bold typography and brand color blocks
- Icons using simple, consistent line weights
